# Color Scan — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

Color Scan respects your privacy. The extension does not require an account or collect personal information for advertising or analytics purposes.

## Information and Data

Color Scan processes visual information from your screen only when you actively use its colour picker to identify a colour.

Selected colours, recent colours, saved colours, palettes and preferences are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage.

Screenshots used by the colour picker are processed temporarily in memory to identify the selected colour.

The extension may temporarily access the active tab's URL to validate the page and support colour picking. This information is not stored as browsing history or transmitted to our servers.

**We do not transmit, sell or share your data with third parties.** The extension does not use analytics, tracking or external data collection services.

## Data Retention

Colour data, saved colours, palettes, preferences and colour history are stored locally in your browser. Extended colour history is limited to 100 entries.

Recent colours can be cleared using the extension. Saved colours and palettes can be removed by the user. Other locally stored data is removed when the extension is uninstalled.
